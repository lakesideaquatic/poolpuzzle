POOL PUZZLE GENERATOR

Weekly lane schedule builder · Mon–Sat

Pool setup

Lane count, bulkheads, and the hours the pool is available each day. For more than one bulkhead, list the lanes separated by commas (for example 8, 16); leave it blank for a pool with none. The generator never places a group across a bulkhead.

Groups

One card per group. Enter the basics, set when the group can be in the water, and choose its days.

How the settings work
  • Auto lanes sizes the group from its numbers (swimmers ÷ swimmers-per-lane, rounded up). Uncheck it to set lanes by hand.
  • Priority runs 1–9: when groups share a time window, priority 1 gets the earliest water and 9 the latest.
  • Length is the whole practice. Tick weight room and the dryland minutes come off the lane time.
  • Earliest start / Latest end bound when the group can be in the water.
  • Auto days: enter days/wk and the generator picks which days (3× lands M/W/F, 4× M/T/Th/F, 6× uses Saturday) and balances the week. Uncheck it and the group swims on exactly the days you check.
  • Two groups with the same coach are never scheduled at the same time.

Other pool operations

Water polo, high school practice, lessons, meets — anything that owns lanes before the puzzle is solved. These are placed first and shown as striped blocks.

Tip: generate, then click any block and use the arrow keys (or the buttons) to move it 15 minutes at a time or shift its lanes — shift+←/→ resizes, delete removes, esc deselects. You can stack groups on top of each other and cross the bulkhead when you need to — anything overlapping gets a ⚠ and is listed under the puzzle, so nothing hides. Removed a group from a day? Put it back with the add a group to… box in that day’s header. Export Excel / Sheets downloads a formatted .xlsx (week-at-a-glance tab plus one tab per day) — open it in Excel, or drop it in Google Drive and open with Google Sheets to get the same colored puzzle. Print gives you the same thing as PDF, one landscape page per day.
